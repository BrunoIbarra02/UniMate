# Per-species action catalogue -- extracted verbatim from animal_creature_3d_animation_catalogue.docx

Source: Downloads/animal_creature_3d_animation_catalogue.docx (Animation convention table). Extracted 2026-11-02 by Claude Code directly from the docx tables via python-docx -- not the PDF (poppler unavailable on this host) and not the graphify graph (which only referenced this doc by filename in CONTEXT.md, never deep-extracted its tables into per-species nodes).

This is the authoritative per-species action list for today's rigged/confirmed quadrupeds. The real production deliverable is the FULL list per species, not just walk/run/jump.

## Dog

idle, walk, trot, run, sprint, sit, lie down, stand up, jump, bark, growl, sniff, dig, shake, scratch, eat, drink, swim, play, attack/bite, recoil, sleep

## Horse

idle, walk, trot, canter, gallop, rear, buck, jump, graze, drink, neigh, paw ground, shake mane, lie down/get up, kick, pull carriage

## Donkey

idle, walk, trot, run, bray, graze, drink, kick, lie down/get up, carry load

## Buffalo/Water buffalo

idle, walk, run, graze, drink, wallow, charge, head-butt, bellow

## Sheep

idle, walk, trot, run, graze, bleat, lie down/get up, head-butt, flock movement

## Goat

idle, walk, trot, run, climb, jump, graze, bleat, rear, head-butt, lie down

## Pig

idle, walk, trot, run, root/snuffle, eat, drink, wallow, squeal, lie down, charge

## Guinea pig

idle, walk, scurry, sniff, eat, drink, groom, squeak, hide, sleep

## Reindeer

idle, walk, trot, run, graze, paw snow, drink, shake antlers, charge, lie down

## Puma/Cougar

idle, walk, run, stalk, crouch, jump, pounce, swipe, bite, growl, rest

## Wolf

idle, walk, trot, run, sprint, stalk, howl, growl, sniff, dig, bite, attack, feed, drink, rest

## Coyote/Jackal

idle, walk, trot, run, stalk, howl/yelp, sniff, dig, bite, feed, rest

## Hyena

idle, walk, trot, run, sniff, laugh/call, growl, bite, feed, drag, rest

## Bear (brown/black/polar)

idle, quadruped walk, run, stand bipedal, roar, sniff, forage, dig, climb, swim, swipe, bite, fish, rest

## Raccoon

idle, walk, run, climb, stand, sniff, forage, manipulate object, wash food, eat, swim, sleep

## Otter

idle, walk, bound, swim, dive, surface, float, roll, groom, hold/eat food, play, sleep

## Wolverine

idle, walk, run, climb, sniff, dig, growl, bite, feed, rest

## Hippopotamus

idle, walk, run, graze, enter water, swim/wade, submerge, surface, yawn/display, bite/charge

## Zebra

idle, walk, trot, gallop, graze, drink, bray, kick, rear, herd run

## Deer/Stag

idle, walk, trot, run, bound, graze, browse, drink, alert, call, antler clash, lie down

## Bison

idle, walk, run, graze, wallow, bellow, charge, head-butt, herd stampede

## Pigeon/Dove

idle, walk, hop, peck, takeoff, flap flight, glide, bank, land, perch, coo, preen

## Seahorse

hover, slow swim, ascend, descend, tail anchor, release, feed/suction, courtship

## Dire wolf

idle, walk, trot, run, stalk, howl, growl, pack attack, bite, feed

## Fenrir/Giant wolf

idle, walk, run, leap, howl, growl, bite, claw, pounce, roar, magical attack

## Werewolf

human idle, transform, beast idle, walk, run, crouch, leap, howl, claw combo, bite, pounce, revert
